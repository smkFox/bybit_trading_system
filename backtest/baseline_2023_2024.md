# EMA Trend Pullback v1 — 2023–2024 baseline

## Status

This is the first unchanged run of the frozen baseline, not an optimized
configuration and not evidence of future profitability. No 2025 or 2026 market
data was inspected to produce this result.

## Reproduction

```bash
python -m backtest.run_backtest
```

## Assumptions

```text
period:                 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31 UTC
starting balance:       10.00 USDT
risk budget per trade:  1.00% of current balance
leverage:               3x
taker fee per fill:     0.055%
slippage per fill:      0.050% adverse
funding approximation:  0.010% cost per crossed 8-hour interval
position limit:         one open position
same-candle SL and TP:   stop-loss first
```

The execution check uses current ETHUSDT constraints observed from Bybit on
2026-07-30: minimum and quantity step `0.01 ETH`, minimum notional `5 USDT`,
and price tick `0.01 USDT`. These values make the 10 USDT feasibility test
realistic for the intended current system, but they are not historical
instrument metadata.

All 922 rejected entries exceeded the configured 1% risk budget after the
quantity was constrained to Bybit's minimum `0.01 ETH`. This is a practical
capital-versus-order-size limitation, not a data or simulator error.

## Baseline decision

The unchanged baseline must not proceed to demo trading. Its expectancy is
negative after modeled costs, and the initial 10 USDT balance cannot express
the intended 1% position sizing for most signals. The next project decision is
whether to test a very small set of explainable alternatives on 2023–2024 or
reject this strategy version.
